Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the management methods of the Studio Legale Puliatti website in relation to the processing of personal data of users who consult it. The information is provided pursuant to the General Data Protection Regulation (EU Regulation 2016/679 - GDPR) and the current Italian legislation.

Data Controller

The Data Controller is Avv. Antonio Puliatti, with headquarters in Viale Vittorio Veneto n. 227, 95127 Catania.

Contact email: segreteria [at] studiolegalepuliatti.com.

Type of Data Processed and Purposes

The website exclusively processes data voluntarily provided by users and browsing data of a technical nature:

1. Data Voluntarily Provided by the User

The optional, explicit, and voluntary sending of electronic mail messages to the addresses indicated on this site (such as segreteria@studiolegalepuliatti.com) or clicking on email links to request information or legal assistance (e.g., in relation to actions for medical specialists or training courses) entails the subsequent acquisition of the sender's address, necessary to respond to requests, as well as any other personal data included in the message.

Legal basis: Processing is based on the execution of pre-contractual or contractual measures taken at the request of the data subject (Art. 6, paragraph 1, letter b, GDPR).

2. Browsing Data

The computer systems and software procedures used to operate this website acquire, during their normal operation, some personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols (e.g., IP addresses, browser type, time of request, etc.). These data are used for the sole purpose of obtaining anonymous statistical information on the use of the site and to check its correct functioning.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest of the controller to ensure the IT security and proper functioning of the site (Art. 6, paragraph 1, letter f, GDPR).

Processing Methods and Data Retention

Data processing is carried out using IT and telematic methods, through suitable tools to guarantee their security and confidentiality. Personal data acquired through direct email contacts will be kept for the time strictly necessary to respond to user requests and for the times provided for by legal and professional ethical obligations connected to the possible legal mandate.

Data Recipients

The data will not be disseminated or communicated to unspecified third parties. They may be communicated exclusively to internal collaborators of the Firm (authorized for processing) or to third-party technical consultants involved in the management and maintenance of the website, appointed as data processors where required.

Rights of Data Subjects

Pursuant to the GDPR (Arts. 15-22), users have the right at any time to exercise their rights concerning data protection:

  • Right of access to their personal data.
  • Right to rectification or erasure (right to be forgotten) of the same.
  • Right to restriction of processing.
  • Right to object to processing for legitimate reasons.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

To exercise these rights, requests should be sent via email to the Data Controller at the email address: segreteria [at] studiolegalepuliatti.com.

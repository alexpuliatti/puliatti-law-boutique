Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Cookie Policy describes how Studio Legale Puliatti uses cookies and similar technologies on its website. The use of cookies is carried out in compliance with European legislation (ePrivacy Directive and EU Regulation 2016/679 - GDPR) and Italian legislation (provisions of the Guarantor for the protection of personal data).

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that the sites visited by users send to their terminals (usually the browser), where they are stored and then retransmitted to the same sites on the next visit. Cookies differ based on their duration (session or permanent cookies) and their function.

Types of Cookies Used by This Site

This website makes extremely limited use of cookies. Specifically, we use:

1. Necessary Technical Cookies

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the site and to allow the user to navigate and use its essential features (for example, saving the light or dark theme preference in the browser).

  • theme: Stores the user's choice for light or dark theme display. This cookie expires automatically or is updated every time the theme switch toggle is clicked.
  • dev-palette: Stores the color palette preference set via the developer selector. It has purely technical purposes.

2. Third-Party Cookies (Maps and Location)

The site integrates an interactive map provided via the MapLibre GL library and public tilesets (CartoDB) to show the geolocation of the Firm's offices in Catania, Rome, and Florence. These services may set technical cookies for smooth map loading or to manage basemap rendering. Studio Legale Puliatti has no access to nor does it perform autonomous tracking on such data.

No Profiling or Marketing Cookies

This site does not use profiling cookies, advertising tracking cookies, or third-party pixels for marketing or commercial remarketing purposes.

Cookie Management and Deactivation

The user can choose to enable or disable cookies by properly configuring the settings of their web browser. Below are links to the instructions for the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: Total deactivation of technical cookies may compromise some minor functionality of the site (such as the persistence of the selected graphic theme upon reloading the page).

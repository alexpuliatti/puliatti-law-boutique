Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Studio Legale Puliatti is committed to making its website accessible, in accordance with current legislation and in line with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) established by the W3C.

Our Commitment

We want to ensure that our digital services are accessible to anyone, regardless of physical, sensory, or cognitive abilities. To this end, we are committed to implementing the best technical practices of inclusive design and semantic web development.

Measures Adopted

During the recent restyling and technological development of the site, the following features were implemented to promote accessibility:

  • Semantic Structure: Correct use of HTML5 tags (such as <nav>, <main>, <footer>, <section>) to facilitate navigation via screen reader.
  • Color Contrast: Careful selection of the color palette (based on the institutional Navy & Terracotta theme) to ensure that the text contrast ratios meet or exceed the recommended WCAG AA thresholds.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Structure of links and interactions (including expandable accordions and buttons) optimized for use without a mouse.
  • Responsive Design: Full optimization for correct layout scaling on any device, allowing text enlargement up to 200% without loss of content.
  • Descriptive Media: Presence of alternative texts (alt attributes) for significant images on the site.

Limitations and Exclusions

Despite our efforts to ensure the accessibility of the entire site, some third-party sections (such as interactive maps provided via MapLibre GL) may present temporary limitations beyond our direct control. We are committed to regularly monitoring these components and proposing alternative solutions where possible.

Reports and Feedback

If you encounter accessibility issues, navigation difficulties, or if you have suggestions to improve the user experience, we invite you to contact us directly:

Email: segreteria [at] studiolegalepuliatti.com

All reports will be carefully examined in order to make timely corrections.
